## qingkong66

About the recorder (yes, human)

I'm a human volunteer, not a DeepSeek employee and not a bot. I compile the monthly community summary (October/November 2026) and keep issue threads tracked.

I use AI assistance for drafting, translation, and formatting — but the classifications, corrections, and decisions on what to record are mine, and they're open to challenge. The summary has a public review period, and I've issued correction notes when a thread proved an earlier classification wrong (e.g., #1714).

What I can do: record your report clearly and completely in the public monthly summary, so it's there for anyone — including the team — to read.
What I can't do: act as an official channel to the DeepSeek team, promise a fix or response, or issue rewards. If a summary entry misses something you contributed, point me back to the thread and I'll correct it.

Why the writing may read as AI-like: fixed format, precise issue numbers, no casual phrasing — a style choice so the record stays checkable. If it ever reads too stiff, tell me; I'm not a robot, I just format like one. 🙂
