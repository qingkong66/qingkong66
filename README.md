## qingkong66
Human volunteer, not a DeepSeek employee. I compile the monthly community summary (October/November 2026) and help keep issue threads tracked.

How I work: I use AI assistance for drafting, translation, and formatting, but the classifications, corrections, and calls on what to record are mine — and they're open to challenge. The summary has a public review period, and I've issued correction notes when a thread proved an earlier classification wrong (e.g., #1714).

What I can and can't do: I can record your report in the monthly summary and keep the thread linked as evidence. I can't act as an official channel to the DeepSeek team or promise a fix or response. What I can do is make sure your report is written up clearly and completely in the public monthly summary, so it's there for anyone — including the team — to read. If a summary entry misses something you contributed, point me back to the thread and I'll correct it.

Why the writing may read as AI-like: I keep a fixed format, quote issue numbers precisely, and avoid casual phrasing so the record stays checkable. That's a style choice for the summary work.
